# Football Know

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform and stack

Web, buildless ES modules and CSS; FastAPI/Pydantic server, private SQLite administrator sessions, independent Python source refresh worker, Caddy HTTPS deployment configuration. No frontend runtime framework or third-party hosting registration.

## Users and purpose

Football readers select one of the twenty current EPL clubs, inspect bilingual player information, recent twenty league results, historical odds, transfers and managerial changes, and study the next match. Two administrators configure the AI provider and local analysis instructions. The owner records personal odds and virtual simulations.

## Current scope

- EPL first; prior Championship league results connect promoted clubs across seasons. Cup and worldwide-league coverage are not claimed.
- Current 672 player names: 628 sourced Mandarin/Hong Kong variants and 44 explicitly unverified editorial phonetic drafts. Source variants always override drafts.
- Match snapshots are checked about once per minute; catalogue and historical changes hourly; Chinese names daily. Polling retains unsaved forms and last-good data.
- Local independent Poisson/shrinkage baseline, explicit sample counts, no trained/calibrated-model or profit claims. Optional administrator-triggered Chat Completions analysis, keys private and never read back.
- Exactly two administrator accounts with independent passwords. Membership, telephone binding and WeChat payments are deferred by the user.
- Decimal home/draw/away odds; virtual bankroll only. Version-1 browser-local personal records and JSON backup remain compatible.
